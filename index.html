NOC Helpdesk

Tiket helpdesk NOC untuk layanan Access, Backbone, Fishbone, dan Other. Formulir lengkap: CID, Link Name, ISP Name, POP, MS/Splitter, MAC Address, Serial Number, VLAN ID, Signal Input CPE (dBm), Latitude, Longitude, Priority, Kanal Pelaporan, dan Problem Description.

  • 16 kebijakan SLA otomatis (4 jenis layanan × 4 priority)
  • Master data: POP, ISP, Link, MS/Splitter, Customer, Kanal
  • Dashboard kepatuhan SLA, deteksi breach, dan timeline tiket
